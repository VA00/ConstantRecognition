# First survey of constant recognition methods on benchmark v0

Runs of 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01; this summary written 2026-10-01. It is a record of the first
thorough comparison and is meant to stay unchanged; later runs go into new files.

## Benchmark

`constants_v0.tsv` as of commit `ba33686`: 1381 real constants, of which the 1348 with at least 17 known
digits are used. Every method gets the same input: the double nearest to the 64-digit value, written
with the shortest digits that round-trip. Every answer is evaluated with 80 digits and compared with the
64-digit value.

- **exact**: the answer agrees with the value to at least 30 digits (or to all its digits)
- **likely** (Wolfram|Alpha only): no expression to evaluate, but the about 20 digits Alpha shows for its
  answer all agree
- **wrong**: presented as exact by the method, but failing beyond double precision
- **false neg.**: right to at least 30 digits, but rejected or not claimed by the method
- **not found**: anything else

Classes as in `constants_v0.tsv` (by the formula in the sources; `none` = no formula in the sources);
class sizes: rational 13, algebraic 133, elementary 402, special 400, none 400.

Notes:
- AskConstants and Wolfram|Alpha answer partly with named constants of their own data (163 of
  AskConstants' exact answers are a single named constant, 111 of them OEIS A-numbers; OEIS and the
  Wolfram Knowledgebase are sources of v0), so they profit from v0 consisting of known constants.
- The methods use different alphabets (their defaults): Constant Recognition CALC4 (13 constants, 18
  functions, 5 operators), RIES its default symbols (no gamma), nsimplify [pi, E, GoldenRatio] and
  integer relations, Maple identify and AskConstants their own.
- Most wrong answers are the same traps for all methods: Ramanujan's pi approximation, the decimal
  selvage numbers, nu = 1 + 1.2e-12, a 50000-term partial sum of pi/2, and the digital tree constant
  1/ln 4 + 1/24 + 1.5e-23. RIES also produces equations that hold only in floating point.

## Methods and settings

| Method | Version | How it was run |
|---------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Constant Recognition, CPU | repository C engine (`C/vsearch_batch.c`, `vsearch_RPN_core.c`), emcc 6.0.9 to WASM, Node.js v22.21.0 | `benchmark/run_benchmark_v0.py --maxk K`; one process per constant, CALC4, relative error <= 16 DBL_EPSILON, shortest K first, no final steps |
| Constant Recognition, GPU | `cuda/constant_gpu_benchmark.cu`, CUDA 13.1 (V13.1.80), sm_120, MSVC of Visual Studio 2022 17.7.6 | `benchmark/run_cuda_v0.py --maxk K`; FP32 search, candidates within 64 FLT_EPSILON verified in FP64, shortest K first |
| sympy nsimplify | sympy 1.14.0, mpmath 1.3.0, Python 3.14.0 | `benchmark/run_nsimplify_v0.py`; `nsimplify(x, [pi, E, GoldenRatio])` |
| Maple identify | Maple 2026.1 (cmaple) | `benchmark/run_maple_identify_v0.py`; `Digits := 16`, `identify(x)` with defaults, 60 s limit |
| Wolfram\|Alpha | via Mathematica 15.0.1 `WolframAlpha[x, {All, "ComputableData"}]`, queried 2026-09-30 | `benchmark/run_wolframalpha_v0.py`; first PossibleClosedForm candidate; x in positional notation |
| AskConstants | 5.0 (D. R. Stoutemyer, MIT), tables installed as .mx, Mathematica 15.0.1 | `benchmark/run_askconstants_v0.py`; `Propose[x, VerboseQ -> False, MaxSearchSec -> 120]`, other options default |
| RIES | 2026 May 06 (R. Munafo, GPL), `ries-for-windows.c` with MSVC `/O2` | `benchmark/run_ries_v0.py --level L`; `ries -F3 -x -lL x`, default symbols; claimed equation solved for x with 80 digits |

`benchmark/compare_results_v0.py` reproduces both tables from the result files in this directory.
